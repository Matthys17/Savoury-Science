Savouring Science

Línea temporal de actividades y tareas del proyecto

Descripción general

To achieve our goal of designing a professional sensory menu, we will follow a structured timeline. This roadmap ensures that we balance scientific research, linguistic practice, and creative design.

The project is divided into four main stages:

Exploration: Researching the psychology of colour and the science of taste using our collaborative Padlet.
The Grammar Challenge: Master the First Conditional through our 'Grammar Escape Room' to be able to explain scientific consequences.
Creative Design: Creating the visual menu using Canva, applying everything we learned about sensory marketing.
Final Showcase: Presenting our menus to the class and receiving peer feedback.
Check the interactive timeline below to see the specific deadlines and tasks for each session. Organization is the secret ingredient of every great scientist!

Línea de tiempo

Tareas: descripción pormenorizada

🟢 Session 1: The Sensory Lab (Research Phase)
"What will we do?" In this first session, you will work in pairs to investigate the fascinating connection between biology and marketing. Using our collaborative Padlet, you must find out how the brain perceives the five basic tastes and, specifically, how certain colours (like blue or red) can trick our appetite.

Goal: Collect at least 3 scientific facts to justify your future menu choices.
Digital Tool: Padlet & Shared Google Doc for note-taking.


🔵 Session 2: The Grammar Escape (Language Mastery)
"What will we do?" Science needs precise language. To explain the 'cause and effect' of sensory marketing, we need the First Conditional. You will work in small teams to solve the riddles of 'The Grammar Escape' Genially. If you solve the puzzles, you will master the structure: 'If I use warm colours, customers will feel hungrier'.

Goal: Complete the Escape Room and obtain the secret code.
Digital Tool: Genially.


🟠 Sessions 3 & 4: Menu Creation (Creative Phase)
"What will we do?" It’s time to be creative! Using Canva, you will design a 3-course sensory menu. You must apply the psychology of colour to the design and write descriptions for each dish using the First Conditional. You need to justify why you chose those ingredients and those specific colours for your brand.

Goal: Design a professional digital menu.
Digital Tool: Canva.


🔴 Session 5: Food Critic Summit (Evaluation Phase)
"What will we do?" In the final session, we will act as professional food critics. You will present your menu to the 'Great Group' and use a Google Form to perform peer evaluation. You will provide constructive feedback to your classmates based on their scientific accuracy and design.

Goal: Peer-assess and reflect on the learning process.
Digital Tool: Google Forms.
