Savouring Science

Task 4 - Food Critic Summit

Descripción y paso a paso

OVERVIEW: Welcome to the final stage! Today, you are no longer designers; you are Professional Food Critics. You will present your work, discuss your classmates' projects, and reflect on your own learning journey.

🍱 The Showcase
Each person will have 3 minutes to present their digital menu to the "Great Group". During the presentation, you must:

Explain the color psychology behind your brand.
Read your First Conditional sentences aloud.
Answer questions from the "critics" (your classmates).


🏃‍♂️ Step-by-Step Instructions:
Present: Open your Canva menu and show your design to the class.
Debate: Engage in a class discussion about which menus were the most "persuasive" and why.
Self-Assessment: Open the "Savoury Science Self-Reflection Form" (Google Forms).
Reflect: Answer the questions honestly.


🎯 Goal
To reflect on the learning process and assess your own progress in English and Science.
