Savouring Science

Instrumentos de evaluación 2: Rúbrica producto final

Descripción general

How is your final menu graded? To help you achieve the best possible results, we use an Analytic Rubric. This document outlines exactly what we are looking for in your final project, from scientific accuracy to digital ethics.

How to use this rubric:

Before you submit: Check your work against the 'Sobresaliente (4)' column. Does your menu explain the 5 basic tastes? Is your typography justified?
Self-Check: Make sure you have used the First Conditional for offers and that all your images are properly attributed.
Continuous Improvement: Use the descriptions in the 'Notable' or 'Aprobado' columns to identify what you need to fix to reach the next level.
Key Evaluation Areas:

Taste Science: Strategic integration of the 5 tastes (including Umami).
Color Psychology: Professional justification of your visual palette.
Grammar Accuracy: Natural use of conditional structures.
Sensory Vocabulary: Use of specific technical terms.
Digital Ethics: Correct attribution and use of Creative Commons licenses.
