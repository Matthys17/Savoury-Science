Savouring Science

Investigación de la ciencia del gusto y psicología del color

Descripción general

Before creating our own sensory menus, we need to understand how our brain perceives flavour and how colour influences our appetite. This stage focuses on curating and analyzing information from expert sources.

Below, you will find two key digital spaces for our investigation:

Collaborative Padlet: A curated wall with videos, articles, and infographics about 'The Science of Taste'. You should explore the resources and choose at least two scientific facts that surprise you to include in your final project.
Digital Toolbox: A repository of the digital tools we are using. This helps us reflect on our learning process and understand the technology behind our research.
