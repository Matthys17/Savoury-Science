Savouring Science

Task 3 - Menu Creation

Descripción y paso a paso

OVERVIEW: It’s time to apply your "Sensory Scientist" skills to a real-world product. You are going to design a 3-course digital menu that is not only delicious but also scientifically designed to attract customers.

📝 The Menu Requirements
Your final design must include:

- A Brand Name & Logo: Based on the color psychology researched in Session 1.
- Three Courses: Starter, Main Course, and Dessert.
- Sensory Descriptions: Each dish needs a short description explaining the flavors and textures.
- The "Scientific Why": Under each dish, you must include a sentence using the First Conditional to justify your design choices. Example: "If I use a yellow background for the dessert, customers will feel more energetic and happy."


🏃‍♂️ Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1 - Draft your ideas: Write down the names of your dishes and the colors you will use.
Step 2 - Open Canva: Choose a "Menu" template. Remember: the layout and colors must match your brand's goal (e.g., healthy, fast food, luxury). Remember that in the section called "Design your Menu (Tutoriales)" within the Padlet you will find a Canva Tutorial and some Menus ideas. You can access the Padlet here. 
Step 3 - Design & Write: Insert high-quality images and write your descriptions. Make sure your First Conditional sentences are grammatically correct!
Step 4 - Export: Save your menu as a PDF or a Public Link to share it in the next session.

🎯 Goal
Design a professional digital menu that uses sensory marketing to influence consumer behavior.


Checklist: Before finishing, check that you have used at least 3 First Conditional sentences.
