Savouring Science

Escape Room

Descripción general

This Escape Room is a comprehensive review of the English conditional system. To succeed in the project, you must demonstrate your ability to use these structures with precision, as they are essential for scientific reasoning and hypothetical thinking.

What will you practice?

Zero Conditional: For universal truths and scientific facts.
First Conditional: For real possibilities and future results.
Second Conditional: For hypothetical or imaginary situations.
Third Conditional: For reflecting on past events and missed opportunities.
Instructions:

Interact: Explore the screens to find the hidden grammar puzzles.
Solve: Apply the correct conditional form to unlock the next stage.
Achievement: Reaching the final screen confirms you have the linguistic level required for the final phase of 'Savoury Science'.
Check your tenses, survivor. One wrong 'if' could change everything!
