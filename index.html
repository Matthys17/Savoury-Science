Savouring Science: The Ultimate Sensory Menu.

Welcome to the "Savouring Science" Challenge! 🥗🧪

Have you ever wondered why fast-food logos are often red and yellow? Or why some flavours make your mouth water while others are simply "meh"?

In this project, you are not just English students; you are Food Scientists and Designers. Your mission is to create a digital menu that is not only delicious but scientifically engineered to trigger emotions and cravings using the Psychology of Colour and the Science of Taste.

The Final Goal: Design a professional menu in Canva that uses the First Conditional to tempt your customers and demonstrates your mastery of sensory vocabulary.

Are you ready to cook up some science?
